Subjective Listening Evaluation for Music Editing

Thank you for participating in this experiment. This study contains 30 MOS evaluation trials and 5 user preference trials.

Please use headphones and complete the test in a quiet environment. Keep the playback volume at a comfortable and consistent level throughout the experiment.

Part I: MOS Evaluation

MOS-P (Source Content Preservation): Evaluate how well the edited result preserves the source music content that is unrelated to the editing instruction. Please consider melody, rhythm, structure, harmony, and other musical content that should remain unchanged.

MOS-T (Target Editing Fidelity): Evaluate how well the edited result satisfies the editing instruction and realizes the intended target musical attribute.

Part II: User Preference Evaluation

Each trial presents two anonymized edited results, A and B. Considering both target editing fidelity and source content preservation, please select the result with better overall performance.

Five-point scale: 1 = Bad, 2 = Poor, 3 = Fair, 4 = Good, 5 = Excellent.
